1938 Edition of the Suśrutasaṃhitā - Facsimile and Transcription

7 June 2026 · Dominik Wujastyk

  • IIIF

This page provides links to the pages of the 1938 printed edition of the Suśrutasaṃhitā (Ācārya & Ācārya, 1938) with parallel transcription of the main text of the work (mūla), without commentaries.

The text is divided according to the arrangement of the chapters in the Nepalese version of the work, which differs from the vulgate in the Uttaratantram.

  • Sūtrasthānam, adhyāyas 1-31
  • Sūtrasthānam, adhyāyas 32-46
  • Nidānasthānam
  • Śārīrasthānam
  • Cikitsāsthānam 1-20
  • Cikitsāsthānam 21-40
  • Kalpasthānam
  • Uttaratantram 1-26 (Śālakyatantram)
  • Uttaratantram 27-37 (Kumāratantram)
  • Uttaratantram 39-24-57 (Kāyacikitsā)
  • Uttaratantram 58-38-66 (Daśakaḥ)
