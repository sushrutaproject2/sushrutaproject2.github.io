- [http://sushrutaproject.github.io/eTexts](https://sushrutaproject.github.io/eTexts/)

provides the searchable texts of:

- _Suśrutasaṃhitā_: [Ācārya's 1938 edition](https://sushrutaproject1.github.io/Sushrutasamhita_Saktumiva2/06-su.ut-27-37-kumara/06-ut-vulgate-1938-27-37.xml?facs=1), with Ḍalhaṇa's commentary.

- _Carakasaṃhitā_: [Ācārya's 1941 edition](https://n2t.net/ark:/13960/t48q2f20n/), with Cakrapāṇidatta's commentary.

- _Aṣṭāṅgahṛdayasaṃhitā_: [Kunte's 1939 edition](https://n2t.net/ark:/13960/t12p44f4h/), with the commentaries of Aruṇadatta and Hemādri.

- _Mādhavanidāna (=Rugviniścaya)_: [Ācārya's 1955 edition](http://n2t.net/%20ark:/13960/s218hd9qx7t), with the commentaries _Madhukośa_ and the _Ātaṅkadarpaṇa_.

- _Śārṅgadharasaṃhitā_: [Śāstrī’s 1931](http://n2t.net/ark:/13960/t7wn11g9r) edition, with two commentaries.
